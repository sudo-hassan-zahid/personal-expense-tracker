# Tharvion product and revenue roadmap

Tharvion's strongest position is not “another expense tracker.” It is a private wealth operating system for people who want clarity without becoming finance experts.

## What to build first

### Tier 1 — features people will return for

1. **Safe-to-spend number** — calculate what is genuinely available after bills, budgets, and savings goals.
2. **Smart recurring detection** — identify subscriptions, rent, salary, and repeating payments; warn before renewals.
3. **Goal vaults** — visual saving goals with target dates, automatic monthly contribution plans, and progress streaks.
4. **Weekly Money Brief** — a short, factual summary of changes, unusual spend, upcoming bills, and one useful action. Keep it deterministic and explainable.
5. **Shared spaces** — household or couple budgets with permissions, comments, and settlement tracking.
6. **Import that feels effortless** — robust CSV templates, duplicate detection, reusable mappings, and localized bank formats.

### Tier 2 — features that justify Pro

1. Cash-flow forecast with “what if?” scenarios.
2. Unlimited accounts, goals, history, exports, and custom categories.
3. Subscription cancellation reminders and price-increase detection.
4. Receipt capture with editable merchant, amount, category, and warranty metadata.
5. Net-worth tracking across cash, debt, property, gold, and investments without pretending to be a trading app.
6. Advanced rules: “transactions matching X go to Y,” split rules, tags, and merchant cleanup.
7. Tax-ready exports for freelancers and side-hustlers.

### Tier 3 — growth and defensibility

1. Local-first or end-to-end encrypted vault mode.
2. Regional packs: currencies, bank importers, tax categories, Zakat calculator, and localized financial calendars.
3. A financial health score whose inputs and recommendations are completely transparent.
4. Accountant/adviser portal with client-approved, read-only sharing.
5. Public API and integrations for payroll, invoicing, and no-code tools.

## Pricing that can work

- **Free:** manual tracking, one budget, two goals, 90-day insights, basic export.
- **Pro ($4–8/month by market):** forecasts, recurring intelligence, unlimited history/goals, advanced exports, receipt capture, rules.
- **Household ($8–14/month):** up to five people, shared spaces, permissions, and settlements.
- **Lifetime launch offer:** useful for early cash flow, but cap the number sold and exclude high-variable-cost features.

Annual plans should be the default. Regional pricing matters more than one global price. Avoid ads and selling financial data; trust is part of the product.

## Sensible launch sequence

1. Ship the brand, onboarding, safe-to-spend, and recurring detection.
2. Add a polished weekly brief and goal vaults; introduce Pro.
3. Add shared households and referral rewards.
4. Build bank connections only after retention proves demand—they add compliance, support, and aggregator costs.

Track activation (first 10 transactions + first budget), week-4 retention, weekly brief opens, goals funded, trial-to-paid conversion, and churn reason. These metrics will reveal more than total sign-ups.
